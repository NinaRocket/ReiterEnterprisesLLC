What to Expect

To help your appointment go smoothly and quickly, here are a few simple things to keep in mind:

Before We Meet

  • Bring a valid photo ID
    A current driver’s license, state ID, or passport is required.
  • Have your documents ready
    Make sure everything is filled out before we meet, except for signatures.
  • Check if you need witnesses
    Some documents require witnesses, so please bring them with you if needed.

At the Appointment

  • Wait to sign your documents
    Please don’t sign anything ahead of time. We’ll do that together during your appointment.
  • All signers must be present
    Anyone signing the document needs to be there with their ID.
  • Know what you’re signing
    I’m here to notarize, not explain legal documents, so please be sure you’re comfortable with what you’re signing.

Final Details

  • Payment is due at time of service
    Please have payment ready. Accepted forms of payment: Zelle, Apple Pay, and cash.
  • Meeting location
    For mobile appointments, please make sure the location is safe and easy to access.
